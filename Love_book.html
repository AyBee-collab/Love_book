By Your Husband
My Dearest Layla
I wish I was talented in a way that I could express how much I love you. I can't paint, sing, dance or write. You settled for this nerd so this is all I have to offer with my limited skill set. I was afraid that I had lost the ability to surprise you. I truly love the privilege of being the one who gets to make you happy. There is nothing more beautiful in this world than your smile. No sound sweeter than your laugh. No touch better than your kiss. This book is my eternal love letter to you. I hope that every day you find yourself closer to seeing yourself the way I see you.
